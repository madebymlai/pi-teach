# SOURCES.md format

`SOURCES.md` is the annotated resource index at the subject vault root. Ground lessons in verified sources from this index.

Group entries by course material, supporting references, and people or communities. Each entry provides a path or URL, what it covers, when to use it, and its limitations. For course material, include exact PDF-page ranges, printed-page labels where needed, and exercise numbers.

The lecturer's material governs exam scope and conventions. Distinguish lecture notes, official exercises, worked solutions, past papers, and generated variants. Preserve original files; label extraction and OCR as derivatives with links to their originals and uncertain passages marked for verification.

Use high-trust supplementary resources to fill declared gaps. Record relevant office hours, study groups, or communities when useful; respect community preferences recorded in `COURSE.md`.

Maintain a Gaps section for missing coverage, unclear scans, and contradictions needing clarification. Annotate reliability corrections so future teaching does not rely on a discredited source. Keep entries concise enough to remain useful after several months.

Originals can live in `Sources/` or remain linked in place. Obtain approval before copying external course directories or sending material to external OCR services.
