# Teaching workspace

This vault contains one subject. Run Pi from its root.

For teaching, study planning, or resuming study after a new session or compaction, read `.pi/skills/teach/SKILL.md` and complete its memory-loading step before selecting work. Course files are the authoritative learning memory.

Use the globally available `ask_user_question` for student preferences and onboarding. The local `quiz` tool provides graded multiple-choice checks in Pi's terminal. Student derivations, proofs, code, and explanations also need direct review.

Keep original materials private and unchanged. Obtain approval before copying or migrating external course directories, or sending their content to external OCR services. Re-read shared notes before editing; preserve student work and superseded learning evidence.
